- 👯 Looking to collaborate on **open-source and full-stack projects**
- 🤝 Looking for help with **my projects & sponsors**
- 💼 Open to **freelance, internships and full-time roles**

- ⚡ Fun fact: **I'm a sociable, open person — say hi!**
